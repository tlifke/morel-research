Read this before the figures. Our run is on harness_val, 40 contracts carved from CUAD's official TRAIN split — the exact data the CUAD paper's three models were fine-tuned on. Their published curve is over the official TEST split, 102 contracts they never trained on.

These are not the same contracts and not the same contract of evidence. A point from ours placed on a curve from theirs would be a memorisation-inflated baseline against an uncontaminated one, on disjoint documents and a different category count (12 vs 41). We therefore do not draw the overlay. The two are shown on identical axes, in separate panels, with the panels explicitly marked non-comparable. The honest overlay becomes available only at gate G4, when test opens and both sides can be computed on the same contracts.

How our point was computed

Not with our own metrics. Per D-26, CUAD's scorer consumes our output unmodified:

  • Gold: data/raw/CUADv1.json read by upstream evaluate.get_answers, subset to our 38-contract intersection × 12 categories — 480 questions in the full 40-contract split, of which 211 carry gold spans and 269 are empty; 336 gold spans total.
  • Predictions: each Extraction(category, spans) becomes their preds_list; each AbsenceClaim becomes []. No threshold is chosen on our side because we do not have one — D-14 commits to one decision per (contract, category), which is exactly their question granularity.
  • Scoring: their compute_precision_recall with their get_jaccard bag-of-words matcher at IOU_THRESH = 0.5, span-level micro-pooled TP/FP/FN, no true-negative cell. Each (contract, category, trial) is a separate question, so three seeds pool exactly as three repetitions. The recomputation was verified to agree bit-for-bit with a direct call to upstream compute_precision_recall.

Two further reasons the numbers are not like-for-like, beyond the split. (a) 12 categories, not 41. Their pooled figures are over all 41; ours are over our 12-category subset, which is a different quantity and must never be printed as the same one. (b) Their matcher cannot see hallucination. get_jaccard is bag-of-words over lowercased, punctuation-stripped tokens, so a paraphrase or a normalised quotation can clear 0.5. Their models extract spans by construction and cannot invent text; ours can, at a measured 5.6% (C2) / 6.0% (C3) not-found rate overall and 14.0% inside C2's false-present cell. Their scorer systematically flatters us; §6 is the correction.

Recall lands at 0.43 for both conditions, comfortably above the 0.2 floor below which the pre-registered reading says the comparison is uninformative — so the machinery is ready for G4. It is the split, not the recall level, that blocks the comparison today.

8. The three findings that outrank the null

a. Truncation is condition-dependent, and it strikes the shortest contracts

4 of 240 trials truncated. All four are C3. Three of four are in the 0–4k bucket, on contracts of 3,385, 4,028, 4,141, 4,005 tokens against a 16,384-token output budget. C2 truncated zero times. Truncation rate is 3.3% in C3 and 0.0% in C2. The citation requirement is what tips reasoning past the budget, and it does so where the document gives least to reason about — which is also the mechanism behind C3's 2.8-point conformance deficit, since truncation lands in json_decode. A pre-run 12-trial budget probe saw zero truncations and peaked at 58% of budget; it did not generalise.

b. Citation frequency does not track principle quality

w01 (619) and w06 (368) take 71% of all citations between them. But w06's applicability checker fires on 1 of 480 harness_val decisions and its gate is gold_absence, and the model cited it 368 times. w10 has no measured footprint at all — no applicability rate, no separability verdict, no phi, never built or footprinted — and drew 91 citations. Models cite readily; a citation count is not evidence a principle did work. The smoke run showed the same pathology, where the two most-cited principles were fabricated calibration controls. Citation correctness remains unmeasured: no applicability source is loaded, the runner reports citation: {available: false} on all 100 scored C3 trials, and nothing here is a citation-accuracy claim.

c. Two categories lose to a trivial baseline, in both conditions

  • Expiration Date scores 0.317 (C2) / 0.337 (C3) presence F1 against an always-present baseline of 0.895. Gold marks it present on 85 of 105 decisions; the model claims absent on 69 of them. A constant "present" would nearly triple the score. This is the single worst cell in the study, and it is a task-definition problem, not a citation problem.
  • Volume Restriction scores 0.100 / 0.000 against an always-present 0.158, with 10 false-presents and at most 1 true-present in either arm.
  • Minimum Commitment sits at parity with its baseline (0.435 vs 0.433). Two thirds of the Savelka confusable trio are therefore at or below trivial. The third, Revenue/Profit Sharing, is the only per-category cell where C3 shows a visible gain (0.678 → 0.787) — on 31 present decisions, uncorrected for twelve comparisons, so it is a hypothesis and not a result.

9. How I read this

The headline is a null, and it is a good one. Requiring the model to name the principles it used changes nothing measurable about what it extracts: presence-class F1 moves +0.0029 [-0.0048, +0.0117], span F1 -0.0075 [-0.0402, +0.0248], exact-match -0.0027 [-0.0601, +0.0512] — fourteen paired comparisons and every accuracy interval straddling zero. What makes it worth having rather than merely uninformative is that the manipulation is demonstrated in the same data: C2 left principles_cited empty on all 1,260 of its decisions and leaked nothing, C3 filled it on 89.8% of 1,200. A null with a treatment you can see landing is evidence; a null without one is a failed experiment. This is the first kind. Concretely it retires the risk the run was scheduled to retire — the citation half of the study can be built without hedging against the requirement degrading extraction — and that means the selection budget downstream buys principle quality rather than insurance.

Two boundaries on what has been shown. The first is the principle set. It is working_set.yaml, not a curated one, and its own records say so: nine of ten principles carry needs_rebuild or not_yet_specified checker status, w10 was never footprinted at all, and several fail the D-21 separability rule outright by gating applicability on gold. C2 already contains all ten principles, so C3 − C2 is the addition of the citation instruction alone, measured at exactly +48 prompt tokens on all 38 paired contracts. This run therefore tests the requirement, not the principles, and nothing here is evidence that principles do or do not help. The second boundary is the CUAD panel. Panel B exists to prove the wiring — their scorer ingests our committed decisions with no invented parameter on either side, and the numbers it returns are sane — but on harness_val, inside their fine-tuning data, at 12 categories rather than 41, it is not a comparison and I have not drawn it as one. When the overlay does become drawable at G4, it will still be a calibration point rather than a hypothesis test: it tells a reader whether our starting position is reasonable or a strawman, which is exactly what makes an unoptimised iteration 0 defensible. The result this study eventually reports is its own iteration-0-to-ladder delta, which shares contamination on both sides and cancels it, and which no comparison to a fine-tuned encoder can substitute for.

Three things in this data would change what I do next, and none of them is the null. The truncation asymmetry is the most actionable defect found: four trials, all C3, three of them on the shortest contracts in the split, is not noise shaped like noise — it says the citation obligation inflates reasoning hardest where there is least to reason about, and it converts directly into C3's 2.8-point conformance deficit through json_decode. Fix it or record it as a known condition-asymmetric cost before the grid, because a manipulation that costs conformance is a manipulation that biases every downstream comparison through survivorship — the mechanism that manufactured a spuriously significant pooled presence-F1 result in this very run, and that a seed-balanced re-analysis reversed. The citation distribution is the second: w06 drew 368 citations while its checker fires on 1 of 480 decisions, and w10 drew 91 with no measured footprint whatsoever, which means citation counts must never stand in as evidence during principle selection. And the third is not about principles at all: Expiration Date at 0.317 against an always-present 0.895, with two thirds of the Savelka trio at or below trivial, is a task-definition problem sitting in the middle of the category subset the study chose on purpose. It will contaminate any per-category reading of a principle effect until it is understood, and it is cheap to look at now.
