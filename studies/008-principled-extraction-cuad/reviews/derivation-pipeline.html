Principle derivation: what changed between round 1 and round 2

A methods reference for the curation gate as a measured object. Round 1 was run and measured on 2026-08-15. Round 2 is designed and instrumented but has not been run; nothing on this page reports a round-2 outcome.

Round 1 — measured 2026-08-15 Round 2 — designed, not yet run Pilot scope: 16 candidates, 5 categories, 1 curator Gate G2 · locked set not yet written

This page exists to make one difference legible: the round-1 pipeline put a human between a model's proposal and a locked principle set with nothing in between, and the round-2 pipeline puts four measurement stages there instead. Every round-2 addition traces to a specific round-1 observation, and that mapping is the argument that the redesign was principled rather than reactive.

Everything here is traceable to methods-scaffold.md §4 / §4a, investigations/002-principle-derivation/investigation.md, plans/decisions.md (D-9, D-19, D-20, D-21), principles/pilot/round1/, and the reports under reviews/. Anything not yet determined is marked open.

Pre-gate measurement band
1 · Checkers implemented and run
Every candidate's checker is coded and run over dev (40 contracts × 12 categories = 480 decisions) before curation. Each candidate arrives with an empirical footprint: rate, in-scope rate, per-category and per-length distribution, a discrimination 2×2, and stability under lexicon variants. Reported in reviews/principle-footprints.md.
2 · Cross-source validation
Each principle is tested against the other source — guideline-derived against gold, data-mined against the Handbook — with outcomes corroborated / contradicted / silent. Over dev + ft_train (404 contracts); holdout never loaded. reviews/cross-source-validation.md.
3 · Adversarial critique
A second model argues against each principle, citing gold, and states its own confidence. The curator adjudicates a disagreement instead of judging a proposition in a vacuum. reviews/principle-critiques.md.
4 · Calibration controls seeded
Principles demonstrably wrong against gold are merged into the round-2 queue in the same schema, register and evidence style, with filled proposer blocks and ids deliberately non-contiguous. The curator knows controls exist; not which they are. reviews/calibration-controls.md.

Human curation gate
Same protocol, same required rationale, same per-field edited_from. Four instrument changes:
+ Inline evidence new
Cited pair ids resolve against the pairs sidecar and render in place: both sides, gold category badges, contract id and offsets, span text, and what the contrast is. Unresolved ids render red rather than vanishing.
+ Footprint block new
Full-width, above every other card: stat tiles, distribution bars, examples. Applicability at or beyond 2%/98% and discrimination lift under 0.05 are flagged amber in place. A missing footprint renders greyed and names the file it looked in.
+ unclear decision new
First-class, separate from defer everywhere: defer = "understood but not rulable yet", unclear = "the statement is not comprehensible". Never pooled in any count or export.
+ Checker-sketch help new

2Why each round-2 addition exists

Every addition traces to a specific thing round 1 did. None of them was added because it seemed like good practice.

Round-1 observation Round-2 addition The mechanism it is exploiting or repairing
Eleven accepts, several resting on "I don't have the domain expertise to disprove this. Accepted." Absence of disconfirming knowledge was being read as support. Checkers implemented and run before curation Converts "is this a good principle?" (needs expertise) into "does this checker discriminate?" (needs none). A principle firing on 0% or 100% of decisions is useless however authoritative its source.
Every confident decision was a cross-source comparison — d01 deferred as contradicting g01, d02 accepted as aligning with g03. Every low-confidence decision was single-source. Cross-source validation, made structural Generalises the one mechanism that actually produced curator confidence. A relation between two independent sources is checkable where the truth of a proposition is not. Silent is expected and is not evidence against: mining is blind to positional categories, and the Handbook is silent on two thirds of the Savelka trio.
The curator was handed assertions, not disagreements, and had no ground on which to contest one. Adversarial critique Adjudicating a disagreement sits at the same lower expertise bar that made the cross-source calls easy. The critic grounds its case in gold and states how strong it believes that case to be.
0 rejects and 0 edits out of 16. A gate that never exercises its negative power may be measuring nothing — and every downstream claim resting on "the set was human-curated" inherits that weakness. Calibration controls Turns rubber-stamping from an intuition into a measured rate. Wrong-against-gold principles are seeded blind; the catch rate makes "the curation gate discriminates" a claim with evidence rather than an assumption.
Evidence rendered as an opaque chip reading pair-0014; the curator deferred d06 saying he couldn't see the pairs. Inline evidence rendering Puts the disconfirming half of every contrast on screen without leaving the app, so single-source judgement is at least as easy as it needs to be.
d08 drew "I have no idea what this means." Under a four-decision vocabulary that had to be logged as a defer. Separate unclear decision Diagnostically different populations: a high defer rate says the evidence pipeline is thin; a high unclear rate says the proposer prompt emits unreadable rules. A comprehensibility failure is a legitimate rejection criterion in its own right.
Round 1 also produced "I don't know what a checker sketch is or what I'm being asked to judge about it." Checker-sketch field help Names the two questions being asked of a sketch, and the accompanying rule that a sound rule with a broken checker is an edit rather than a reject — which is exactly the distinction the edit-rate analysis needs to survive.

Deliberately not added, and the reason is methodological. Nothing that pushes the gate toward rejection was implemented: no "strongest counter-evidence" panel next to the accept button (the app would have to pick, which is the tool arguing for rejection with a mechanism the writeup cannot describe), no running accept-rate readout framed as a rate (counts inform; a rate with an implied acceptable value nudges), and no extra friction on accept specifically (asymmetric friction manufactures the result it is trying to detect). An interface that pushes toward rejection is as much a measurement error as one that pushes toward acceptance, and it corrupts the same number.

The one flow change, rationale first, is off by default and applies identically to all five decisions.

The gate discriminates over structural properties, not substantive ones

This is the sharper claim the round-1 record supports, and it is not "the human gate is worthless".

WHERE IT WORKED

Contradiction and corroboration

d01 was deferred as directly contradicting g01; d02 was accepted as aligning with g03. Both relations were detected without domain expertise, because a relation between two sources is checkable where the truth of a proposition is not.

Every confident decision was a cross-source comparison. Every low-confidence decision was single-source.

WHERE IT WORKED

Evidence strength

Both defers were correct on the merits. d07 was deferred asking for more support — independent verification later found its main clause refuted (D-20: 204 furniture-split counterexamples against 25 supporting spans, replicated by a second independent regex family). d06 was deferred, and the proposer had already conceded it sat below its own evidence bar (effective n=1).

Two genuinely weak candidates were caught by a curator with no contract-law background.

WHERE IT WORKED

Comprehensibility

d08 drew "I have no idea what this means" — a comprehensibility failure of the principle rather than of the curator, and a legitimate rejection criterion in its own right. Round 1 had no vocabulary to record it as one.

WHERE IT DID NOT

Substantive truth

Accept-by-default under uncertainty. g02 was accepted as "another clear principle direct from the guidelines" — and it is one; the Handbook genuinely prescribes the convention. It is also provably inert: the <omitted> marker occurs 0 times in 404 contract texts and 0 times in the raw CUAD v1 JSON, because CUAD encodes discontiguity as multiple spans. Being true was never the issue.

The expertise limitation, stated plainly. D-9 describes curation as expert-derived or expert-curated in the general case. This study substituted a non-expert curator. The round-2 additions are the designed response to that constraint, not a post-hoc excuse — and this is the most contestable part of the method, so the writeup pre-empts it rather than defending it later.

The five deferred records are the highest-information output of round 1 and should not be presented as a backlog that was cleared. In particular the d01-vs-g01 contradiction is a disagreement between our two derivation methods. (Cross-source validation later found it is not a real contradiction: g01's own trigger guidance exempts the date categories, which is exactly d01's scope, and the Handbook draws that line itself. The record's defect is that its statement is phrased globally while its scope field is narrow — a drafting artifact, fixable by an edit.)

4The three decouplings

Each is stated as X did not predict Y. Each undercuts a heuristic a reader would otherwise assume, and together they are the case for the round-2 instrumentation. All measured on the pilot's 16 candidates: checkers over dev (480 decisions), cross-source validation over dev + ft_train (404 contracts), and independent adversarial review. These are the most transferable findings on this page.

DECOUPLING 1

Provenance did not predict quality

Of the 8 guideline-derived principles, one is unimplementable and two are tautological. Of the 8 data-mined, one is refuted, one fires once in 480 decisions, and one fires on nearly everything.

The two best footprints are one from each arm (g04, d04). The curator's acceptance rate, by contrast, was source-dependent: 7/8 guidelines against 4/8 mined.

Confidence tracked source authority; quality did not. This is the sharpest single justification for replacing source-based judgement with measurement.

DECOUPLING 2

Evidence strength did not predict measurement value

d05 has the strongest derivation evidence in the set — 8 mined pairs across 4 contracts, 6 of them same-contract contrasts, 4 near-verbatim minimal pairs — and the adversarial critic could not break its main clause.

Its footprint is among the worst in the set: it is applicable on 75% of in-scope decisions and fires on 100% of contracts over 4k tokens. A quantity plus a bound cue is ambient legalese. It cannot discriminate at that rate.

Best-evidenced is not most-measurable. The two questions are independent and only the second one determines whether a principle can carry the mediation analysis.

DECOUPLING 3

Statement truth did not predict checker usability

Adversarial review could not break 5 of 16 statements — but only 2 of those 5 had checkers it also could not break.

d04 is the extreme: the best-evidenced statement in the pilot, attached to a regex that reaches 27% of the spans it is about and misses the second-largest span class entirely.

A principle can be true and unmeasurable. "Is this a good principle?" decomposes into two independent questions, and the statement/checker gap is where most of the damage was. This is why checkers now precede curation (D-21).

Two independent instruments converged. The applicability footprints and the adversarial critique were built for different purposes and reached the same disqualifications — g02 unimplementable, d07 and d06 dead, and the tautology family. Convergence of methods built for different purposes is worth reporting as corroboration rather than as redundancy.

5The three hard constraints on entry to the scored set

A candidate that survives curation still has to clear all three. Two were in the protocol from the start; the third was forced by adversarial review of the pilot.

1 · No feasible checker or labeling plan → excluded

The checker is sketched at proposal time, not afterwards. A principle with no feasible (instance, gold) → bool test does not enter the scored set, however sound the rule reads.

Pilot casualty: g02. Accepted in round 1 as a clear Handbook-derived principle, and it is one — but its applicability condition is satisfied 0 times out of 404 contracts, so no model behaviour can ever move it.

2 · A principle the schema already enforces is unmeasurable

The harness guarantees output coverage (exactly one decision per target, D-14) and validates structure before scoring. A candidate that merely restates a schema guarantee — "emit a decision for every category", "cite at least one principle" — shows 100% compliance in every condition by construction, contributing nothing to mediation while quietly inflating the compliance pass-rate.

Screening test: if you cannot describe an output that is schema-valid yet violates the principle, the principle is not scoreable.

3 · Compliance must be separable from correctness  D-21

A principle enters the scored set only if a model can pass its checker and still be wrong, and fail it while still being right. Screening is mechanical: populate the 2×2 of {passes checker, fails checker} × {answer right, answer wrong} over dev; a structurally empty off-diagonal disqualifies.

Why a tautological checker destroys the analysis. The chain the study reports is principles → compliance → success, with compliance as the mediation variable. If passing the checker and getting the answer right are the same event, a C2/C3 compliance gain cannot be evidence that principles work through compliance — the mediation coefficient is not merely noisy, it is unidentifiable. The causal claim degenerates into a restatement of answer accuracy.

Pilot casualties: 6 of 16 checkers. Two gate applicability on gold.is_impossible, so the checker asks the answer; four define compliance as emitting the gold answer. Every one of them read as sound prose. The defect lives entirely in the checker — which is precisely why checkers must be implemented and inspected before curation, not after.

Worse, the two gold-gated ones are structurally incapable of registering their own cost: they only look at instances where absence is correct, so the false absences they would induce elsewhere never enter the compliance number at all.

Constraint 3 is the reason the pipeline changed shape, not just gained stages. Constraints 1 and 2 can be applied by reading a record. Constraint 3 cannot: it is a measurement over dev, so it forces the checker-implementation stage to sit before the gate rather than after it. Round 1's ordering made it structurally impossible to enforce.

6The calibration-control instrument, in general terms

Enough to cite in methods without compromising the blind. No control is identified anywhere on this page, and the answer key is not to be opened until round-2 decisions are recorded.

Principles that are demonstrably wrong against gold are seeded into the round-2 queue in the same schema, register, level of detail and evidence style as real candidates, with filled proposer blocks, and renumbered so they do not form a contiguous block. Each was disproved against dev + ft_train before entering the set — candidates whose wrongness could not be demonstrated were discarded during authoring, because a control whose wrongness cannot be shown is indistinguishable from a real principle that happens to be wrong, and would corrupt the measurement in the direction that flatters the gate. Holdout was never loaded. Difficulty is tagged per control across an easy / medium / hard spread, deliberately: an all-easy set makes a perfect catch rate uninformative, an all-hard set makes a zero uninformative.

Scoring rules, fixed in advance

outcomedefinition
catchreject, or an edit that removes the wrong claim
missaccept
abstentiondefer — reported as its own category and never counted as a catch. Round 1 shows defer is this curator's response to uncertainty, not to detected error; folding defers into catches would let the gate score well by being uniformly unsure.

Three numbers get reported, not one — overall catch rate, catch rate by difficulty tag, and abstention rate — alongside the false-alarm rate on real candidates, because a curator who rejects everything catches every control while adding no information.

Open, and it must be recorded on the record before scoring. The difficulty tags depend on which of two conditions round 2 actually runs. If controls are curated raw, the tags stand and the instrument measures unaided detection. If controls receive the same cross-source and critique pass as real candidates, expect them all to surface pre-flagged, and the instrument instead measures whether the curator acts on a contradiction flag — under which a miss is a much stronger negative result, and the difficulty tags must be reported as not applicable rather than reused. The two are not comparable, and the difference is larger than any effect this instrument can resolve at this sample size.

Limits carried with any result: the sample is small and the binomial intervals are wide — the instrument is powered to separate "near zero" from "substantial", not to estimate a rate, so the difficulty-stratified reading is the primary result and the overall rate is secondary. It tests detection of wrongness, not recognition of value. It says nothing about whether good-but-unobvious principles survive — the round-1 defers are exactly that population. It is one curator, one round, one queue. And once round 2 is scored the controls are burnt: a round-3 measurement needs new ones, and the measured catch rate should be expected to rise for reasons that have nothing to do with the gate's quality.

No control may reach the locked set. After scoring all are removed regardless of their review decisions, including any that were accepted — an accepted control is the finding, not a principle.
